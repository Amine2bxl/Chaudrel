# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Homeowners in Brussels and its periphery (and more widely Belgium) planning a whole-home or whole-apartment renovation with a comfortable budget. They compare a few contractors, look for proof that the work is well finished, and want to know who will run the job. Secondary: people with a single job (kitchen, bathroom), and architects or interior designers who may recommend Chaudrel.

## Product Purpose
Showcase site, portfolio and quote-request tool for Chaudrel Rénovation (Schaerbeek, founded 2009). Success = qualified quote requests (`/devis`), phone calls and WhatsApp messages from owners with complete renovation projects.

## Positioning
Two founders run every job themselves: Alberto (site and technique) and Matteo (design and client relationship). One contact from the quote to handing back the keys; Chaudrel coordinates every trade.

## Operating Context
Visitors arrive from Google (local search, Google Business Profile), Instagram/TikTok/Facebook, word of mouth and printed material (business card QR to `/devis?utm_source=carte`). Leads are then qualified on WhatsApp, followed by a site visit and a detailed quote.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- React 18 + Vite 5 + Tailwind 3, every route prerendered to static HTML, deployed on Vercel. `/api/lead` receives quote requests (Resend; 503 fallback shows phone and WhatsApp).
- Content lives in `src/data/*`. French only (fr-BE).
- Brand kit (logo, business card, t-shirts, Google Business profile, documents) lives in `marque/` (generated by `studio/`, the reusable kit for the next clients), separate from site code.

## Brand Commitments
- New logo supplied by the client (pending upload); it drives the new visual identity.
- Client brief for the redesign: harmonious, structured, impactful, real value; quality animations from established animation libraries; ultra-clean animated glassmorphism.
- Copy may be rewritten freely for impact, but nothing invented.

## Evidence on Hand
- Founded 2009 (17 years), two founders, headquarters Rue Henri Stacquet 49-51, 1030 Schaerbeek, VAT BE0812283245.
- Promises confirmed: reply within 48 h; free quote with no commitment.
- Real project photos in `public/photos/` (Overijse, Uccle, Ixelles, Tervuren, Woluwe), some with before/after pairs.
- Not validated, never display: project counts, satisfaction rates, Google rating, price ranges, durations, ten-year guarantee, the six testimonials in `src/data/testimonials.js` (`TESTIMONIALS_VALIDATED = false`). Hours are provisional. See `docs/VERIFICATION.md`.

## Product Principles
1. Proof before promise: finished work shown large comes before any claim.
2. One contact, said and shown: the two founders are the reason to choose Chaudrel.
3. Zero invention: every number, review or delay on the site is validated by Chaudrel.
4. Every page ends on an easy next step: quote, call or WhatsApp.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA; `prefers-reduced-motion` must neutralise all motion.
