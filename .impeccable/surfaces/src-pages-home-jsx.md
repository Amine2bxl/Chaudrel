# Home (/) — surface brief

Scope: home page, and the shared chrome (nav, footer, contact dialog) every route inherits. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: homeowners planning a whole-home renovation (Brussels, Belgium). Action: request a free quote (/devis), call, WhatsApp.
Proof: real job photos (exteriors: doors, frames, facades, roofs), founders since 2009, reply within 48 h. Nothing invented.
Constraints: logo-pinned palette and type; client brief = ultra-clean animated glassmorphism, library animations (Motion + Lenis). Hero photo is a stock ambience image, never captioned as a Chaudrel job.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The logo's bronze door is the entrance to the site: the visitor walks through it into a finished home. Refuses the category default of a full-bleed house photo with headline and three buttons on top.

OWN-WORLD: Night #191714 and cream #FCF4EC in alternating fields, warm ink #24221E, bronze #AE8A56 as the only accent (doors, actions, rules). Prata for display, Albert Sans for everything else, wide-tracked caps echoing RÉNOVATION. Glass is the window material: frosted panels over photos and night fields, cream hairline edge, inner top highlight.

STORY: Understand what Chaudrel does and who runs it, believe it through real jobs and the founders' single point of contact, act by asking for the quote.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Night field, cream CR monogram centred at about 45% width with the bronze door; headline in Prata below; a glass action bar holding Demander un devis gratuit (bronze), phone, WhatsApp. Scrolling opens the door: monogram scales through the door, the door becomes the photo, which straightens to full-bleed; a glass card carries the promise and actions.

FORM: door-opening scroll sequence, position 1 on my ordered list; dealt card 3 of 3, picked by the user; seed:3efc11159446ef3f
